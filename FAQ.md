## Getting Started

**Do I need to create an account?**

No. 77 Chores does not require a separate account. The app stores your data on your device and, if enabled, syncs it through your existing iCloud account.

**What is the setup process?**

Setup walks you through six steps in order:

1. Name your household
2. Add household members
3. Choose which chores your household tracks
4. Assign each chore to the person responsible for it
5. Set your rotation length
6. Build your rotation by deciding which chores happen on which days

You can explore the app using a sample household before completing your own setup.

**Do I have to complete setup all at once?**

You can return to finish setup later. The app saves your progress as you go.

**What is the chore library?**

The chore library is a built-in list of common household chores. During setup you select the chores your household wants to track. Only the chores you select appear in your rotation.

**What does "assign chores" mean?**

During setup you decide which household member is responsible for each chore. This determines whose turn it is to schedule each chore when building the rotation, and whose name appears next to it.

## The Rotation

**What is a rotation?**

A rotation is a fixed, repeating sequence of days — for example, 14 days. You decide what chores happen on each day of that sequence. When the sequence ends, it starts over from day one. 77 Chores always shows you the chores scheduled for wherever you are in the current cycle.

**How is this different from a to-do list?**

A to-do list grows over time as tasks accumulate. The rotation is a plan you design in advance. Each day's chores are fixed by the rotation; the app does not automatically add missed chores to future days.

**How long should my rotation be?**

It depends on your household's rhythm. Common choices are:

- **7 days** — a one-week repeating schedule
- **14 days** — a two-week cycle, useful for alternating weeks
- **21 or 28 days** — three- or four-week cycles

You can also set a custom length between 1 and 60 days. You can change the length later if it isn't working.

**What do the different chore frequencies mean?**

When you select a chore, it has a suggested frequency — for example, daily, weekly, or monthly. This tells the app how often the chore fits into a typical rotation:

- **Daily** — placed on every day of the cycle automatically
- **Weekly** — placed on the same weekday in each week of the cycle
- **Several times a week** — can be placed on multiple days
- **Monthly, quarterly, or annually** — placed on a single day in the cycle

**How does "Build Your Rotation" work?**

In the final setup step, you tap each day of the rotation to choose which chores happen on that day. When chore responsibilities have been assigned, each household member takes a turn selecting days for their own chores. When everyone is done, the rotation is complete.

**What if I put no chores on a day?**

That is allowed. A day with no scheduled chores is simply a rest day — nothing will appear in the daily view for that position in the cycle.

## Daily Chores

**How do I see today's chores?**

When you open the app, the Today view shows the chores scheduled for the current day of your rotation. The app determines the current day by counting forward from the rotation's start date.

**How do I mark a chore as done?**

Tap a chore to mark it complete.

**How do I see more details about a chore?**

Long-press a chore to open its detail view.

**What if today has no scheduled chores?**

If no chores are assigned to today's position in the rotation, the Today view will reflect that. You can review upcoming chores or check the rotation to plan ahead.

## Completing and Skipping Chores

**What is the difference between completing and skipping a chore?**

Completing a chore records that it was done. Skipping records that you intentionally chose not to do it on this occurrence — for example, you are away or circumstances changed. Both are recorded in your history.

**Can I undo a completion?**

Tap the chore again to change its status.

**Can I mark a chore for a past date?**

77 Chores focuses on the current day's plan. Editing records for past dates is not a supported workflow.

## Unfinished Chores

**What happens if I don't complete a chore?**

Nothing automatic. The chore remains in your history as incomplete for that day. It does not move to tomorrow's list and does not create a running backlog.

**The app didn't remind me that yesterday's chore was unfinished. Is that a bug?**

No. 77 Chores is built around a planned rotation, not a catch-up system. Unfinished chores stay in history so you can review them, but they do not accumulate or roll forward automatically. If you want to do a chore outside its scheduled day, you can do it and the rotation simply continues as planned.

**How do I catch up on something I missed?**

The app does not have an automatic catch-up mechanism. If a missed chore matters, you can do it on the next available day. If you find yourself consistently missing a chore, consider adjusting the rotation so it falls on a more realistic day.

## History

**Where can I see what has been completed?**

The History view shows a record of completed, skipped, and incomplete chore occurrences.

**How far back does history go?**

History is stored on your device and in iCloud alongside the rest of your household data. There is no automatic limit described in the app.

## Changing Your Rotation

**Can I change my rotation after setup is complete?**

Yes. The rotation editor lets you change which chores appear on which days. Tap into any day in the rotation to add or remove chores.

**Can I change the rotation length?**

Yes. If you shorten the cycle, days beyond the new end are removed. If you lengthen it, new empty days are added. Any chores already scheduled on retained days are preserved.

**Can I change which chores the household tracks?**

Yes. You can add or remove chores at any time. Removing a chore also removes it from all days in the rotation.

**Can I change who is responsible for a chore?**

Yes. Chore assignments can be updated in the rotation settings.

**What should I do when my household routine changes significantly?**

You can edit the rotation incrementally — adjusting individual days — or reconfigure it more broadly by revisiting the rotation settings. There is no need to start over from scratch unless you want to.

## Household Members

**Do household members each need their own device?**

Not necessarily. One person can manage the household on a single device, or the same iCloud-synced data can appear across multiple devices belonging to the same Apple Account. See [iCloud and Your Devices](#icloud-and-your-devices) for details.

**Can different people in my household use the app on their own separate Apple Accounts?**

Not currently. 77 Chores does not support sharing a household between people who have different Apple Accounts. Sync works within a single Apple Account across that person's own devices.

**What is the turn-taking during "Build Your Rotation"?**

When chores have been assigned to members, the Build Your Rotation step gives each member a dedicated turn to select which days they want to do their chores. This makes it easy to pass a single device around the table during setup so each person can set their own schedule.

## Sample Mode

**What is the sample household?**

Before completing your own setup, you can explore a pre-built sample household called The Maplewood House. It demonstrates how the rotation, Today view, and history work in practice.

**Does using the sample affect my real household data?**

No. The sample is completely separate from your own household. Changes you make in the sample do not affect your setup, and resetting your real data does not affect the sample.

**Can I reset the sample to its original state?**

Yes. The sample can be reset to its original pre-built state at any time.

**Can I convert the sample into my real household?**

No. The sample is for exploration only. Your real household is set up separately through the setup flow.

## iCloud and Your Devices

**Does the app use iCloud?**

Yes, if iCloud is available on your device. 77 Chores stores your household data as files in your iCloud Drive. If iCloud is not available or not enabled, the app works entirely from local storage on your device.

**Do I need to do anything to turn on iCloud sync?**

Ensure iCloud Drive is enabled in your iPhone or iPad's Settings under your Apple Account. If it is enabled when you first run the app, sync starts automatically.

**Which of my devices will have my household data?**

Any iPhone or iPad signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled will sync your household data.

**What happens if I make changes on two devices at the same time?**

The app merges changes where it can — for example, if you add a member on one device and add a chore on another, both changes are preserved. For the rotation structure itself, the most recently modified version wins. In practice, conflicts are uncommon when one person manages the household.

**What happens if iCloud is temporarily unavailable?**

The app continues working from the local copy on your device. Changes are synced when iCloud becomes available again.

## Troubleshooting

**My chores are not syncing between devices.**

1. Check that both devices are signed into the same Apple Account.
2. Check that iCloud Drive is enabled on both devices in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Drive.
3. Check that both devices have a working internet connection.
4. If the issue persists, open and close the app on both devices to prompt a sync check.

**The app is showing the wrong day of the rotation.**

The app determines today's position by counting forward from the rotation's start date. If the start date is set incorrectly, the rotation will be off. Open the rotation settings, check the start date, and adjust it to match the real first day of your cycle.

**A chore I deleted is still showing up.**

If you have multiple devices syncing, the deletion may not have propagated yet. Wait a moment and then pull to refresh or close and reopen the app.

**The app is showing "No Rotation Configured."**

This appears when setup has not been completed through the rotation-building step. Return to the setup flow and complete the remaining steps.

**I completed a chore by mistake.**

Tap it again to change its status.
