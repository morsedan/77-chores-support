# Privacy Policy for 77 Chores

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

77 Chores ("the App") is a household chore rotation app developed by Emerald Gateway Solutions ("we," "us," or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## Information You Provide

The App allows you to create a household, add household members, select chores, configure chore rotations, and track chore completion.

Depending on how you use the App, this information may include:

- Household names
- Household member names
- Chore selections and rotation schedules
- Chore completion, skipped, and incomplete history
- Other information you enter into the App

## How Your Information Is Stored

The App stores household information locally on your device and can synchronize that information across your own devices through Apple's iCloud services.

We do not operate a separate account system or an app-owned server for storing your household information.

iCloud synchronization is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's terms, privacy policy, and iCloud settings.

Household information is not currently shared between different people's Apple Accounts. Synchronization is intended for your own devices using the same Apple Account and the applicable iCloud configuration.

## Information Collection and Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account with Emerald Gateway Solutions to use its core features.

We do not use household information for advertising.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Household information is retained in the App's local storage and, where enabled, in iCloud.

You can manage your household information through the App and your device's available storage and iCloud settings.

Deleting the App from a device may not remove copies of information that have already synchronized to iCloud or other devices. Consult Apple's documentation for information about managing and deleting iCloud data.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general household use and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a separate account or data collection system.

If you have questions or concerns about information associated with the App, contact us using the information below.

## Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple's operating system and iCloud services for applicable storage and synchronization features. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and terms.
