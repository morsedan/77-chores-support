# 77 Chores Support

77 Chores helps you build a repeating household chore rotation that fits your life. Plan which chores belong on which days, balance heavier and lighter days, and focus on today's plan without automatically carrying unfinished chores forward.

If you need help using the app or have encountered a problem, use the information below.

## Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, or help with 77 Chores, email:

**77chores@77apps.dev**

When reporting a problem, please include:

- A brief description of the issue
- The steps you took before the problem occurred
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead
- Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version
- The app version, if available

Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information in your message.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the chore rotation work?

You create a repeating schedule by deciding which chores belong on which days. The rotation repeats according to the cycle you configure, allowing you to distribute work across your household's schedule.

### Do unfinished chores automatically move to the next day?

No. 77 Chores does not automatically carry unfinished chores forward and add them to the next day's plan. Each day follows the rotation you designed. You can review chore history and intentionally change your recurring schedule when needed.

### Can I skip a chore without completing it?

Yes. You can skip an individual occurrence when appropriate. Skipped, completed, and incomplete occurrences can be distinguished in chore history.

### Can I change my rotation after setting it up?

Yes. You can deliberately edit your recurring plan when your household's needs or schedule change.

### Can I try the app before setting up my household?

Yes. The interactive sample household lets you explore how the rotation works before configuring your own household.

### Does my household data synchronize across devices?

Household data can synchronize across your own devices through iCloud when configured and available. The app does not require a separate 77 Chores account.

### Can different household members use separate Apple Accounts to share the same household?

Not currently. iCloud synchronization is intended for your own devices using the same Apple Account. The app does not currently provide shared household data across different people's Apple Accounts.

### What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app removes it from your device, but it does not necessarily remove information that has synchronized to iCloud or remains on other devices. Review your Apple Account and iCloud settings to manage synchronized information.
